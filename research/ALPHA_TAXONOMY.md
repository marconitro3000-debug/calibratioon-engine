# Alpha Taxonomy

## Cross-sectional alphas

- price reversal
- momentum
- volume-price interaction
- volatility reversal
- liquidity proxies
- relative value proxies
- residual/factor-neutral signals

## Time-series alphas

- trend following
- mean reversion
- volatility breakout
- realized-volatility signals
- regime-conditioned signals

## Statistical arbitrage alphas

- pairs trading
- cointegration spreads
- PCA residuals
- basket residuals
- Kalman hedge-ratio spreads

## Microstructure alphas

- order imbalance
- spread capture
- short-term reversal
- adverse-selection filters

## Alpha rejection reasons

- weak out-of-sample IC
- high turnover
- excessive cost drag
- high beta/sector exposure
- high correlation with existing book
- unstable parameter region
- single-period dependency
- excessive multiple-testing risk
